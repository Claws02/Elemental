# Elemental: full audit (2026-10-30)

Read-only audit at `d03218e` (branch `claude/elemental-game-design-os46tu`). Models were rendered headless (gallery, every kingdom's town); story read from `docs/STORY.md`, `PROGRESSION.md`, `CHECKLIST.md` and the scene builders in `scripts/scenes/`. Contact sheets: `gB.png` (buildings), `gC.png` (creatures), `gP.png` (people), `gT.png` (towns) in the session scratchpad.

Severity: **P0** breaks the story or the player's path · **P1** reads wrong to a player · **P2** polish.

---

## 1. Where it stands

- Built and tested: prologue (Veyra) → Lesson I (Oruun Gate) → Thornwick / Dry Mill → training yard → Watchstone → Sunken Cistern (Lesson II) → Saltmere sea wall → Cold Forge (Lesson III). About 2–3 hours of authored story.
- Blocked out with no script: Emberwall, Skyreach, Glass Expanse, Halcyra (`script: none` in all four).
- Systems are strong: 4 elements + 6 combinations, destructible structures by material, ledger and standing, saves, 12 creatures, terrain, a test per system in CI.
- Missing entirely: **audio**, native iOS/Android projects, a device benchmark.

## 4. Why is that house there? (placement)

**The root cause:** `town()` in `scripts/scenes/lib/region.mjs:60` places every building on a ring at equal angles, all facing one point. Thornwick, Cindrel, Sarn and Vaelmont all come out as the same clock face (screenshot `gT.png`). Nothing in a town says why it grew where it did.

**Fix: place buildings by reason, not by angle.** Replace the ring with a small rule set, each rule a reason a person would give:

| Building | Goes where | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Hall | Head of the plaza, on the highest ground, facing the main road | Seen by everyone arriving |
| Smithy / forge | On the road in, near water | Deliveries, quenching, noise away from homes |
| Mill | On the river (Thornwick's already is) | It needs the current |
| Barn | Edge of town toward the fields | Hay goes in from the field side |
| Houses | Along the roads, doors to the street, gaps for lanes | Towns grow along roads |
| Market | Where roads meet | Traffic |
| Inn | At the road's entry | Travellers |
| Well | Plaza centre | Shared |

Per town:

- **Thornwick:** a river-market town, so the stalls belong by the dock and the bridge, not in a ring. The ring barn belongs out by the north-east fields.
- **Cindrel:** a caldera city. Show *why* anyone lives there: lava channels running from the vents to each `cindrel_forge`, houses in terraces up the bowl wall, the Queen's hall over the hottest vent.
- **Sarn:** houses on the oasis's shaded side, tents downwind, caravan pens on the road out.
- **Vaelmont:** terraces stepping up to the abbey, cells cut along the cliff, the glider launch at the windward edge.
- **Halcyra:** a capital needs districts: walls, a gate, a market quarter, the Lantern Office, the palace island, and **an eastern district** (Act III says you tore it up). That's the biggest single authoring job.
- **Veyra:** already reads as a village. It's the best one.

**Tooling:** the brief already asks for HBD's `qa/zfight.js`. Add a placement lint alongside it: every building's door faces a road or plaza within N metres, no door faces a wall or a cliff, nothing is buried or floating.
